Zing

Zing is a private assistant app for Android, made by one person for his own family. It is not sold and has no ads.

It runs its language model on the phone itself. When you ask, it can read your Gmail or Hotmail inbox on the phone to summarise the day's emails for you. It does not send, delete or move email without your explicit OK and your fingerprint.
